# Country Content Guide

## Purpose

This guide defines how country content should be curated and written.

The curation quality is the product.

We are not trying to fill a database with technically correct facts. We are trying to create cultural experiences that make people want to keep going.

## Core Test

For every recommendation, ask:

`Would I tell a friend about this?`

If the answer is no, the recommendation is not strong enough.

The failure mode to avoid is:

- correct
- respectable
- generic
- forgettable

That kind of content is not enough for this app.

## What A Great Country Entry Should Make Someone Feel

After exploring a country, the player should feel curious enough to:

- listen to something
- cook something
- read something
- look something up
- want to go there

If they only feel like they learned five facts, the entry is not working.

## Curation Rules

AI and editors should not default to:

- the most obvious landmark
- the most tourist-board recommendation
- the safest textbook example
- the first culturally correct thing that appears

Instead, recommendations should feel:

- genuinely curated
- emotionally sticky
- conversation-worthy
- alive
- worth revisiting

## Balance Rule

Every country bundle should collectively balance:

- `Iconic`
- `Everyday`
- `Contemporary`
- `Artistic`
- `Unexpected`

This balance should happen across the full bundle, not necessarily inside every single entry.

Example:

- one item may be iconic
- another may feel intimate or everyday
- another may be modern and current
- another may be visually or artistically rich
- another may surprise the player entirely

## V1 Experience Types

The five V1 country experiences should be framed as verbs:

- `TASTE`
- `LISTEN`
- `READ`
- `SEE`
- `CREATE`

These are not just category names. They define the emotional posture of the reveal.

### TASTE

Food that makes someone want to make it, try it, or imagine eating it.

### LISTEN

Music people may genuinely want to keep listening to, not just historically important recordings.

### READ

Excellent books by authors from that country. They do not need to be about the country.

### SEE

Places worth picturing or wanting to visit:

- landscapes
- neighborhoods
- monuments
- museums
- streets
- natural wonders
- districts

### CREATE

Creative work that widens the portrait of the country:

- artists
- art
- design
- sculpture
- architecture
- photography
- craft

## Tone

Write like a smart, culturally curious friend with high taste.

Good tone:

- concise
- vivid
- admiring
- stylish
- warm

Avoid:

- textbook tone
- tourism brochure tone
- generic “this country is known for”
- overexplaining
- flattening an entire country into a symbol

## Structure Of A Strong Entry

Each country should include:

- one reveal intro
- three fun facts
- one `TASTE` entry
- one `LISTEN` entry
- one `READ` entry
- one `SEE` entry
- one `CREATE` entry
- capital and region metadata
- clue prompts that already teach something

## Clue Rules

Mixed clues are part of the product experience, not just gameplay scaffolding.

A clue should already leave behind:

- one image
- one dish
- one artist
- one landmark
- one useful association

Even if the player guesses incorrectly, the round should teach something memorable.

Good clues:

- evocative
- fair
- short
- culturally meaningful
- not so obvious that they feel flat

## TASTE Rules

Choose dishes that are:

- delicious-sounding
- culturally meaningful
- concrete
- worth cooking, ordering, or searching for

Good `TASTE` entries should not read like encyclopedia summaries. They should create appetite.

## LISTEN Rules

Music should balance significance and replay value.

The question is not only:

`Is this artist important?`

It is also:

`Would someone actually want to press play?`

When possible, balance:

- one classic or foundational artist
- one contemporary or later-facing artist

## READ Rules

Books do not need to summarize the nation.

They should be:

- excellent
- inviting
- worth reading on their own

Choose books because they are strong books by authors from that country, not because they can be forced into a culture lesson.

## SEE Rules

Replace the narrow idea of `Monument` with a broader place concept.

Good `SEE` picks may include:

- a mountain
- a city district
- a coastline
- a cave system
- a market
- a museum
- a neighborhood
- an archaeological site

The right `SEE` pick is whatever makes the country feel vivid.

## CREATE Rules

This category should widen the player’s sense of the country’s creative life.

Strong candidates include:

- a painter
- a sculptor
- a designer
- a photographer
- a studio
- a movement
- an architectural figure

The test is:

`Does this make the country's creative world feel real and alive?`

## Fun Facts Rules

Fun facts should be:

- short
- varied
- memorable
- non-cliché when possible

They should support the mood without becoming filler.

## Development Sequence

Do not mass-generate all 12 launch countries yet.

The correct order is:

1. Build architecture for all 12 launch countries.
2. Deeply curate Lebanon, Japan, and Mexico first.
3. Test the emotional experience.
4. Only then scale the content system.

These first three are the benchmark set.
